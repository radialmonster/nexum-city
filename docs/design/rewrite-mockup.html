THE MAP IS A WINDOW ONTO A GROWING CITY

Meridian is bigger than one screen and it grows as crews join. You pan and zoom across it; the three zoom levels below are the same city at different distances. On the main screen above, try the + / − buttons and the CTY · TAC · DST levels beside the minimap.

Zoom 1 · City TERRITORY BLOBS

Who holds what. Every district is a coloured blob. The dashed fringe is the frontier, where new plots open and new crews are placed. Pings mark your alerts and the newest arrival.

Zoom 2 · Tactical THE DEFAULT

Where you give orders. Streets, convoys, siege rings, market prices and the edge of your sight. Districts carry on past every edge of the window.

Zoom 3 · District CLOSE VIEW

What is inside. Building slots, walls going up, the warehouse filling, crews at the gates and carriers parked and ready.

OTHER SCREENS & UI PIECES

These panels slide in over the map. They never replace it. Labels marked INSTANT happen right away. Labels marked TIMED become orders on the timeline.
